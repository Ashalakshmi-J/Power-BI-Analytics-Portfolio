### 🔍 Core Analytics Modules Engineered
1. **Average Salary by Job Title (Horizontal Bar Chart):** 
   - Tracks incoming compensation tiers across specialized roles, identifying top earners across Data Scientists, Data Engineers, and Architecture pipelines.
2. **Country of Survey Takers (Donut Distribution Chart):**
   - Visualizes geographic market penetration, highlighting leading response volumes across the United States, India, United Kingdom, Canada, and competitive global regions.
3. **Favorite Programming Language (Vertical Column Chart):**
   - Measures developer ecosystem preferences, contrasting the adoption margins of Python, R, SQL, JavaScript, and C++.
4. **Average Salary Based on Gender (Radial Gauge Chart):**
   - Aggregates comparative gender compensation benchmarks, tracking an overall combined execution performance index of 73.17.
5. **Quality of Life Indexes (Twin Gauge Meters):**
   - Formulated operational sentiment tracking scales to evaluate employee workplace comfort:
     - *Happiness Meter Based on Current Job Salary:* 4.27 / 10
     - *Happiness Meter Based on Work/Life Balance:* 5.74 / 10
6. **Difficulty to Break into Data by Gender (Area Chart Grid):**
   - Maps sociological career entry boundaries, detailing professional friction thresholds ranging from 'Very Easy' up to 'Very Difficult' segmented by gender populations.
